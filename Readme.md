## 🚀 About Me 
I'm a Data Analyst who enjoys turning messy data into clear insights and meaningful stories.

I work with data to uncover patterns, understand customer behavior, evaluate business performance, and help turn numbers into actionable decisions.

I enjoy building dashboards that make data easy to explore and understand, as well as improving repetitive reporting and data preparation processes through automation.

I'm especially interested in the connection between data and business — understanding not just what happened, but why it happened and what we can learn from it.

I love the "aha!" moment when data reveals something that wasn't obvious at first.
